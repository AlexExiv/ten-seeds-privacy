Need help with Ten Seeds? Please check the answers below or contact us by email.

Contact

Email: support@speakerboxlite.com

Please include your device model, operating system version, game version, and a short description of the problem. Do not include passwords or payment details.

Game progress, leaderboards, and achievements

Sign in to Google Play Games on Android or Apple Game Center on iOS. Make sure you use the same account on every device. If progress does not appear, check your internet connection, restart the Game, and confirm that the game service is signed in.

Purchases and removing ads

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. If ads return after reinstalling the Game or changing devices, sign in with the account used for the original purchase and use the restore-purchase option. You will not be charged again for restoring an eligible purchase.

Troubleshooting

  • Update Ten Seeds and your device operating system.
  • Restart the Game and then restart your device.
  • Check that you have a stable internet connection.
  • If the problem continues, email us with the steps that reproduce it.

Privacy and data

Read the Ten Seeds Privacy Policy for information about data collection, third-party services, and data deletion.
