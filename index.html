Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aleksei Abdulin (“we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you play Ten Seeds (the “Game”) on Android, iOS, and web. By playing the Game, you agree to this Policy.

1. Accounts and sign-in

We do not create accounts and never ask for your name, email, phone number, or password. The Game can connect to Google Play Games Services (Android) or Apple Game Center (iOS) so you can use leaderboards, achievements, and saved progress. That sign-in is handled by Google or Apple with your existing account; we never see your password. You can play without signing in — those features are simply unavailable.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Device information: model, operating system and version, language, screen, and general device settings.
  • Identifiers: advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) and app-instance/analytics identifiers.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (country/region level), not precise GPS.
  • Gameplay and usage data: sessions, scores, progress, in-game events, and settings.
  • Diagnostics: basic performance data.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown and interacted with.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

3. Game services: leaderboards, achievements, saved progress

If you sign in to Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center:

  • We receive a player identifier from that service, and your public gaming profile name may be displayed.
  • Scores you submit appear on leaderboards and are visible to other players under your gaming profile.
  • Achievements you unlock are recorded against your account.
  • Your game progress may be saved to your Google or Apple account (cloud saved games) so you can continue on another device.

This data is stored by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies and account settings, where you can review or delete it. You can always play without signing in.

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers an optional one-time purchase that removes ads. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store — we never receive or store your card number, billing address, or payment credentials. We receive only confirmation that the purchase was made, so the Game can disable ads and restore the purchase on your devices. Purchase records are also held by Google or Apple under their own policies.

5. How we use information

  • To run, maintain, and improve the Game and its balance.
  • To analyze usage and measure performance (analytics).
  • To provide leaderboards, achievements, and saved progress when you sign in.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To display advertising, including personalized ads where you have consented.

6. Third-party services

We use the following services, which collect and process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics (analytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • GameAnalytics (game analytics) — gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Game Center and the Apple App Store (iOS) — apple.com/legal/privacy

These providers may act as independent controllers of the data they collect.

7. Advertising

The Game shows ads via Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier. You can opt out of personalized ads and reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking / Apple Advertising). On iOS, we request permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt before using your IDFA for tracking. For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, we show a consent request before serving personalized ads. If you buy the ad-removal purchase, we stop showing ads in the Game and no longer collect advertising data for that purpose.

8. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on: your consent (for personalized ads and, where required, analytics); performance of a contract (to deliver and restore in-app purchases and, if you sign in, game services); and our legitimate interest (for basic functionality, security, and aggregate analytics). You may withdraw consent at any time.

9. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 and is not intended for a child audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If the Game is available to a mixed audience, users identified as children are served only non-personalized ads and are excluded from tracking. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data retention

We keep analytics and diagnostic data only as long as needed for the purposes above, after which it is deleted or anonymized in line with our providers’ retention policies. Purchase confirmations are kept as long as needed to honour and restore your purchase.

11. Your rights and data deletion

We do not operate accounts and do not keep our own database of players. The data described above sits with our providers and is tied to device identifiers rather than to your identity — from an email we cannot determine which data relates to you, and we are not required to collect additional information solely for that purpose.

You can delete most of this data yourself:

  • Advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings, where you can also turn off personalization. After a reset, earlier data is no longer linked to your device.
  • Leaderboards, achievements, cloud saves: these belong to your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account. Deleting your Play Games data for this game (or your Game Center data) removes your achievements, progress, and leaderboard scores. We cannot delete an individual score on your behalf.
  • Purchases: managed in your Google or Apple account.

Depending on your region (EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or object to processing. Write to support@speakerboxlite.com — we will respond, do what is within our control, and forward the request to the relevant provider.

12. International transfers

Data may be processed on servers outside your country, including by our providers. They apply safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

13. Data security

We and our providers use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date, and continued play constitutes acceptance.

本隱私權政策說明當您在 Android、iOS 及網頁端遊玩 Ten Seeds（下稱「本遊戲」）時，Aleksei Abdulin（下稱「我們」）如何蒐集與使用資訊。遊玩本遊戲即表示您同意本政策。

1. 帳號與登入

我們不會建立帳號，也絕不索取您的姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼或密碼。本遊戲可連接 Google Play 遊戲（Android）或 Apple Game Center（iOS），讓您使用排行榜、成就與已儲存的進度。該登入由 Google 或 Apple 以您既有的帳號完成，我們絕不會看到您的密碼。您也可以不登入即遊玩，只是上述功能將無法使用。

2. 自動蒐集的資訊

  • 裝置資訊：型號、作業系統及版本、語言、螢幕以及一般裝置設定。
  • 識別碼：廣告識別碼（Android 的 Google Advertising ID、iOS 的 IDFA）以及應用程式執行個體／分析識別碼。
  • 約略位置：依您的 IP 位址推斷（國家／地區層級），並非精確 GPS。
  • 遊玩與使用資料：工作階段、分數、進度、遊戲內事件與設定。
  • 診斷資訊：基本效能資料。
  • 廣告資料：曾顯示哪些廣告以及您曾與哪些廣告互動。

我們不會有意蒐集精確位置、聯絡人、相片或麥克風資料。

3. 遊戲服務：排行榜、成就與已儲存進度

若您登入 Google Play 遊戲或 Apple Game Center：

  • 我們會自該服務取得玩家識別碼，且可能顯示您的公開遊戲個人檔案名稱。
  • 您提交的分數會出現在排行榜上，其他玩家可在您的遊戲個人檔案下看到。
  • 您解鎖的成就會記錄於您的帳號中。
  • 您的遊戲進度可儲存至您的 Google 或 Apple 帳號（雲端存檔），以便在其他裝置上繼續遊玩。

這些資料由 Google 或 Apple 依其各自的隱私權政策與帳號設定儲存，您可在其中查看或刪除。您隨時可以不登入即遊玩。

4. 應用程式內購買

本遊戲提供一次性的選購項目以移除廣告。付款完全由 Google Play 或 Apple App Store 處理——我們絕不會接收或儲存您的卡號、帳單地址或付款憑證。我們僅接收購買已完成的確認資訊，以便本遊戲關閉廣告並在您的裝置上還原購買。購買紀錄同樣由 Google 或 Apple 依其政策保存。

  • 用於營運、維護及改善本遊戲與其平衡性。
  • 用於分析使用情況並衡量效能（分析）。
  • 用於在您登入時提供排行榜、成就與已儲存的進度。
  • 用於處理及還原應用程式內購買。
  • 用於顯示廣告，包括在您同意的情況下顯示個人化廣告。

我們使用下列服務，其依各自的隱私權政策蒐集及處理資料：

  • Google AdMob（廣告）— policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics（分析）— firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • GameAnalytics（遊戲分析）— gameanalytics.com/privacy

這些提供者就其蒐集的資料可能以獨立控管者的身分行事。

7. 廣告

本遊戲透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。廣告可能依您的廣告識別碼進行個人化。您可以在裝置設定中選擇停用個人化廣告並重設廣告識別碼（Android：設定 > 隱私權 > 廣告；iOS：設定 > 隱私權與安全性 > 追蹤／Apple 廣告）。在 iOS 上，我們會先透過 App Tracking Transparency 提示請求許可，才會將您的 IDFA 用於追蹤。對於歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者，我們會在放送個人化廣告前顯示同意請求。若您購買移除廣告，我們將停止在本遊戲中顯示廣告，並不再為此目的蒐集廣告資料。

在適用 GDPR 的情況下，我們依據：您的同意（用於個人化廣告，以及必要時的分析）；契約之履行（用於提供及還原應用程式內購買，以及您登入時的遊戲服務）；以及我們的正當利益（用於基本功能、安全與彙總分析）。您可隨時撤回同意。

本遊戲並非以未滿 13 歲之兒童為對象，亦非為兒童受眾所設計。我們不會有意蒐集兒童的個人資料。若本遊戲提供予混合受眾，被判定為兒童的使用者僅會看到非個人化廣告，並排除於追蹤之外。若您認為某位兒童提供了資料，請與我們聯絡，我們將予以刪除。

我們僅在上述目的所需期間內保存分析與診斷資料，之後將依提供者的保存政策刪除或匿名化。購買確認資訊將於履行及還原您的購買所需期間內保存。

我們不營運帳號，也不保存自己的玩家資料庫。上述資料存放於我們的提供者處，且關聯的是裝置識別碼而非您的身分——僅憑一封電子郵件，我們無法判斷哪些資料與您相關，也沒有義務僅為此蒐集額外資訊。
